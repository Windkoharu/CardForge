包含「圣痕」怪兽的战士族怪兽×3
这张卡不能作为融合素材。
这张卡用融合召唤以及以下方法才能特殊召唤。
●把自己场上的上记的卡除外的场合可以从额外卡组特殊召唤。
①：1回合1次，以对方场上1张表侧表示的卡为对象才能发动。那张卡的效果直到回合结束时无效。这个效果在对方回合也能发动。
②：1回合1次，对方把怪兽的效果发动时，从卡组把1只战士族怪兽送去墓地才能发动。那个发动无效并破坏。
③：这张卡被战斗·效果破坏的场合才能发动。从自己墓地选1只「圣痕龙骑士 露米艾尔」以外的战士族怪兽特殊召唤。

--- en ---
3 Warrior monsters including a "圣痕" monster
Cannot be used as Fusion Material.
Must be Fusion Summoned or Special Summoned by the following method.
● Special Summon this card from your Extra Deck by banishing the above cards from your field.
① Once per turn: You can target 1 face-up card your opponent controls; negate the effects of it until the end of the turn. This is a Quick Effect.
② Once per turn, when your opponent activates a monster effect: You can send 1 Warrior monster in your Deck to the GY; negate the activation, and if you do, destroy it.
③ If this card is destroyed by battle or card effect: You can Special Summon 1 Warrior monster other than "圣痕龙骑士 露米艾尔" from your GY.
